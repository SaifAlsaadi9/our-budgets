What this is

Our Budgets is a personal, self-hosted budgeting application operated by an individual for their own use. It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and is not a commercial product.

Access

Use is restricted to the owner. Access is protected by a password; sharing that password or attempting to access the application without authorisation is not permitted.

What it does with bank data

With the owner's consent, given at their own bank, the application retrieves account balances and transaction history through Enable Banking, a regulated account information service provider. Access is read-only. The application cannot initiate payments, transfer funds, or alter anything held at the bank.

Consent lasts 90 days and can be withdrawn at any time in the application or through the bank's own permissions screen.

Not financial advice

Figures shown here — balances, category totals, debt payoff estimates and projected dates — are informational calculations based on the data available and the assumptions entered. They are estimates, not advice, and not a statement of account. Your bank's own records are always authoritative. Do not rely on this application for tax, lending, or any other financial decision without checking the underlying accounts.

Accuracy

Transaction data comes from third parties and may be delayed, incomplete, duplicated, or miscategorised. Automatic categorisation is a best guess based on merchant descriptions and is frequently wrong. The owner is responsible for reviewing what is imported.
